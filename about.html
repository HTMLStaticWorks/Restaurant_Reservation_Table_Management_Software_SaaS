The Origin Story

Why We Built This Platform

The restaurant industry was shackled by predatory per-cover fees and fragile legacy technology. We set out to change the game.

✕

The Legacy Platform Trap

For years, big tech platforms charged restaurants $1.00 to $2.50 for every guest seated — even regular diners who already knew the restaurant. An operator seating 2,500 covers a month lost over $45,000 yearly just for booking software.

  • Exorbitant cover fees: Draining restaurant profit margins on repeat regulars.
  • Host stand crashes: Bulky desktop software freezing during 7:30 PM Friday dinner rushes.
  • Held-hostage guest data: Platforms remarketed diners to competitors down the street.
✓

The TableFlow Philosophy

We built TableFlow with a simple premise: restaurants own their guests, keep 100% of their revenue, and deserve modern, lightning-fast cloud software with zero per-cover penalties.

  • Flat transparent subscription: Zero per-cover charges regardless of volume.
  • Real-time cloud speed: Synchronized across iPad, terminal, and phone in under 50ms.
  • 100% guest ownership: Full direct access to phone numbers, emails, and diner notes.
